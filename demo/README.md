# Dashboard Demonstration

## Purpose

The demonstration shows how the Power BI report responds to date,
country, product-group, and review-status selections.

It also explains why unusually large positive entries should be
investigated before interpreting product or customer rankings.

## Open the Report

Download [E-commerce_Performance.pbix](../report/E-commerce_Performance.pbix)
and open it in Power BI Desktop.

A static overview is available in the
[PDF report](../docs/E-commerce_Report.pdf).

## Suggested Recording

Target duration: 60–90 seconds.

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 0–10 seconds | Show Sales Overview with all countries and review statuses selected |
| 10–20 seconds | Select United Kingdom and show recalculated values |
| 20–30 seconds | Change the date range to 1 January–30 November 2011 |
| 30–40 seconds | Open Product Performance |
| 40–50 seconds | Select High sales / high units |
| 50–60 seconds | Clear the portfolio group; restore all countries and full observed dates |
| 60–70 seconds | Open Customer Analysis |
| 70–82 seconds | Select Other positive lines in Review status |
| 82–90 seconds | Clear the review selection and return to Sales Overview |

Reset date and country filters before the review-status comparison.
The investigated invoices are UK records dated 18 January and
9 December 2011.

## Narration

This report explores sales, product demand, and customer value in
the UK Online Retail dataset.

The overview shows approximately £10.64 million in recorded positive
value and £10.26 million in named-merchandise sales. Negative entries
are excluded, so these figures are not net revenue.

Selecting a country and changing the date range recalculates the
cards and charts.

The product page compares sales with units. Portfolio groups describe
full-period performance, while the displayed measures respond to
the selected filters.

After restoring the baseline, the customer page shows approximately
£8.89 million associated with identified customers. The top five
contribute 11.8% of that value.

Two exceptionally large positive invoices have matching negative
entries recorded minutes later. The review-status filter lets viewers
inspect their effect on customer value and rankings.

Clearing the filter restores the baseline. The report combines
interactive analysis with documented data-quality decisions.

## Interpretation

Excluding the two investigated positive invoices is a sensitivity
comparison. It does not incorporate all returns or establish net sales.

## Recording Preparation

- Restore all baseline filters before starting.
- Use Fit to page and keep navigation visible.
- Collapse editing panes where practical.
- Close unrelated applications and notifications.
- Move the pointer deliberately and pause after selections.
- Preview the recording for readable labels and clear audio.
